# Third-party material

lcd-spec adapts text and ideas from these projects:

| Source | License | What was used |
|--------|---------|---------------|
| [obra/superpowers](https://github.com/obra/superpowers), Copyright (c) 2025 Jesse Vincent | MIT | brainstorming, writing-plans and subagent-driven-development processes; the implementer, task-reviewer, re-review, final-reviewer and plan-reviewer prompts (condensed and adapted to OpenSpec) |
| [OpenSpec](https://github.com/Fission-AI/OpenSpec), Copyright (c) 2024 OpenSpec Contributors | MIT | the `spec-driven` schema's artifact instructions and templates for proposal, specs and design |
| [mattpocock/skills](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills), Copyright (c) 2026 Matt Pocock | MIT | the grilling procedure (one question at a time with a recommendation; facts looked up, decisions asked) |
| [howdeploy/Z.A.E.B.A.L.](https://github.com/howdeploy/Z.A.E.B.A.L) | no license file at the time of writing | **ideas only**: frustration as an audit signal, weighted streak with three levels, two independent auditors fed raw artifacts, FACT/HYPOTHESIS tagging, named failure patterns. No text or code was copied; `references/incident-audit.md`, `scripts/frustration.py` and the lexicons are written independently. |

The pre-apply audit (`references/stage-5-audit.md`) comes from the author's own
`preplan-audit` skill. The deep-reasoning directives in the perspective reviewers
(`prompts/perspective-reviewer.md`) come from the author's own `megathink` skill.

The MIT license texts of the sources above apply to the adapted portions.
